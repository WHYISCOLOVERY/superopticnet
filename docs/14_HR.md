# 14 HR

## Purpose

HR manages employees, roles, permissions, schedules, attendance, vacation, field work and KPI.

## Required Functions

- Employee create/edit with staff login.
- Role and permission assignment.
- Telegram chat ID for Superpilot/bot access.
- Shift and work schedule.
- Attendance: present, absent, late, leave and field duty.
- Vacation/leave tracking.
- Ticket performance.
- Inventory assets assigned to employee.
- KPI dashboard and reports.

## Employee Roles

- Super admin.
- Billing operator.
- NOC engineer.
- GPON engineer.
- Technician.
- Inventory manager.
- Finance/accounting.
- Customer support.
- HR manager.
- Security operator.

## KPI Inputs

- Tickets assigned, resolved and delayed.
- Customer satisfaction/complaints.
- Field task completion.
- Inventory handover/return accuracy.
- Attendance.
- Alarm response time.
- Billing operation accuracy.

## Safety

- Staff login must be separate from public customer login and must not expose sensitive internal wording publicly.
- Every employee action is permissioned and audited.
- Superpilot can assist employees but must respect role permissions.
